About Me

Hi, I’m Rahis, a passionate designer from Kanyakumari. I completed my Higher Secondary Education in 2025 and have since been focused on building my creative and technical skills. My journey started with a strong interest in editing and graphic design, which gradually evolved into web design, app design, and logo creation. I genuinely enjoy transforming ideas into visually appealing and user-friendly digital experiences. Once I start a project, I dedicate myself fully to delivering high-quality results. I am especially passionate about UI/UX design, where creativity meets functionality. I believe that great design is not just about appearance — it’s about creating smooth, meaningful experiences for users. My father is a fisherman, and watching his hard work and dedication has shaped my mindset from a young age. It has taught me responsibility, consistency, and the importance of staying committed to my goals. Beyond design, I am also a State-Level Football Player and a District-Level Javelin Throw Athlete. Sports have strengthened my discipline, focus, and teamwork — qualities that I bring into my professional journey as well.
